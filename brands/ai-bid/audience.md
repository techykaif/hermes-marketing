# AI-Bid — Audience

## Core audience

Builders and businesses that want visibility for products in a competitive discovery environment, beginning with AI and Games.

## Priority segments

1. Solo AI builders
2. Small AI product teams
3. Indie game makers and small game teams
4. Visitors actively discovering new AI products or games
5. Builders seeking measurable product visibility rather than generic promotion

## Pain signals

Prioritize conversations about:

- difficulty getting initial product visibility
- crowded AI-tool directories
- launching without an audience
- discovering promising new tools
- finding differentiated products
- wanting measurable visibility or ranking

## Buyer intent signals

Strong signals include active launches, requests for discovery/visibility, directory comparisons, product promotion questions, or explicit interest in paid ranking/discovery mechanisms.

## Messaging principles

Explain the ranking/discovery mechanic clearly. Never imply that a paid bid guarantees users, sales, a specific ranking, or editorial endorsement.
