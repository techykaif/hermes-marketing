# Keanso — Product Knowledge

## Verified identity

- Product: Keanso
- Positioning: invoice follow-up workspace for freelancers and small businesses
- Core promise: Get paid without chasing.
- Core workflow: create invoice → track payment status → follow up at the right time → get paid.

## Primary users

- Freelancers
- Independent professionals
- Consultants
- Small businesses and small teams
- People who regularly send invoices and manually follow up on unpaid work

## Core capabilities

- Workspace and onboarding
- Client management
- Invoice ledger and lifecycle tracking
- Actionable billing queue
- Payment claims and administrative review
- Automated transactional invoice reminders
- Client/invoice communications
- Wall/community surface with moderation
- Admin control plane with privileged actions and auditability

## Product philosophy

Keanso is intentionally a focused financial-operations workspace rather than a generic accounting suite. Marketing should emphasize simplicity, clarity, professional follow-up, and reduced manual chasing.

## Launch focus

The documented launch focus is reliability and correctness of the core product, especially admin action reliability, billing/invoice separation, payment-state integrity, moderation, safe client deletion, final audit, and production testing.

## Explicitly deferred / future

Do not market these as current launch capabilities unless verified separately:

- Invoice void workflow
- Stripe billing
- Client portal/payment links
- Expanded analytics
- Other non-critical feature expansion

Future monetisation idea: optional email reply copies with conversation history, with entitlement gating deferred until validated by real usage.
