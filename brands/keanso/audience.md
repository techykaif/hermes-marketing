# Keanso — Audience

## Core audience

People and small teams that send invoices and lose time following up on unpaid work.

## Priority segments

1. Freelancers and independent professionals
2. Consultants and service providers
3. Small agencies
4. Small businesses with recurring client billing

## Pain signals

Prioritize conversations and research showing:

- unpaid invoices
- late payments
- awkward payment follow-ups
- manual invoice tracking
- forgotten follow-ups
- cash-flow pressure caused by slow-paying clients
- time lost chasing clients

## Buyer intent signals

High-value signals include someone actively asking for tools, workflows, templates, automation, or advice for managing unpaid invoices and payment follow-up.

## Messaging principles

Lead with the concrete problem and outcome. Avoid exaggerated claims such as guaranteed faster payment or guaranteed recovery of overdue invoices.
