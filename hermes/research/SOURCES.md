## Source precedence

1. Live production behavior
2. Current repository implementation
3. Current product documentation / implementation status
4. Current product requirements
5. Historical documents

A lower-ranked source must not be used to assert a capability contradicted by a higher-ranked source.

## Research source classes

### First-party

Product websites, repositories, release notes, implementation-status documents, public API documentation, official analytics exports, and official platform documentation.

### Market

Competitor websites, public pricing pages, public launch pages, public changelogs, industry publications, community discussions, and search results.

### Social/community

Public posts and discussions on relevant networks. Use them for pain-point and opportunity discovery, not as proof of product capabilities.

## Evidence requirements

Each material finding should capture:

- source URL
- source type
- observed timestamp
- product/market affected
- concise finding
- confidence: high / medium / low
- whether the finding is directly verified or inferred

Do not treat a single unsourced social post as a market fact.
