# Daily Marketing Operations Skill

## Mission

Turn the approved strategy and current intelligence into safe, measurable marketing work for Keanso and AI-Bid.

## Required workflow

1. Load the latest `reports/latest/strategy.md`, brand product/audience files, and marketing policy.
2. Check today's prior execution log to avoid duplicate work.
3. Select only tasks that are explicitly executable under current policy.
4. Produce channel-specific content that is truthful and appropriate to the audience.
5. Prefer drafts/queued actions until publishing integrations are explicitly configured.
6. Record every attempted external action and result.
7. Record performance signals when available.

## Content rules

- One clear idea per post.
- Match the channel's normal format and audience.
- Do not invent customer stories, testimonials, numbers, or outcomes.
- Do not imply endorsements or guaranteed results.
- Use current product capabilities only.
- Avoid repetitive copy across channels.

## X/Twitter safety boundary

Research and content drafting are allowed.

Do not autonomously:

- mass-like posts
- mass-follow accounts
- mass-unfollow accounts
- send unsolicited promotional replies or mentions
- send unsolicited DMs
- manufacture engagement
- use multiple accounts to manipulate visibility

Only publish through an approved official API/integration and only within explicitly configured permissions.

## Approval boundary

Human approval is required for:

- paid advertising or spending
- new external partnerships
- sensitive claims
- claims involving revenue, customers, conversion, or performance numbers without a verified metric source
- high-volume outreach
- any action whose platform-policy status is uncertain

## Daily output

Write a concise execution record under `reports/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md` containing:

- strategy items selected
- content created
- actions executed
- actions skipped and why
- errors
- metrics observed
- next action

If no safe action is available, record `NO_EXECUTABLE_ACTIONS` rather than forcing activity.
